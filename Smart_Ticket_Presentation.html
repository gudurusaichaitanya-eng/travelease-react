01 — Project Overview

Abstract

A quick, simple summary of our Smart Ticket Booking Platform

⚠️

The Problem Today

Right now, people have to download separate apps to book flights, trains, movies, and concerts. This is confusing and wastes phone storage.

During big festival rush or flash sales, websites crash, seats get double-booked, and scammers sell fake screenshot tickets to multiple buyers.

💡

Our Smart Solution

SmartTicket combines all travel and event bookings into one single, clean website.

When a user selects a seat, our system locks it for 7 minutes so nobody else can take it. It generates a secure digital QR code ticket directly on your phone that cannot be copied or faked.

🚀

Why It Helps Everyone

Users enjoy fast booking with instant UPI payments (PhonePe, Google Pay), quick ticket transfer to friends, and 1-click easy refunds.

Organizers stop ticket fraud, eliminate double bookings, and see live passenger count and earnings in real-time.

CORE PROMISES
No Double Bookings  •  Fake-Proof QR Tickets  •  All Bookings in One App  •  Instant Easy Refunds
02 — The Real Problems

Problem Statement

Four big challenges in existing ticketing systems that we solve

📱

Too Many Different Apps

Users have to switch between 4 to 5 separate apps for flights, trains, cabs, and events. There is no single place to see all tickets.

⏱️

Seat Booking Conflicts

When thousands of people book at the same second, websites glitch and two customers accidentally pay for the exact same seat.

🛡️

Fake & Screenshot Scams

Scammers take screenshots of PDF tickets and sell them to multiple people. Innocent buyers get rejected at the entry gate.

🔄

Hard to Cancel or Get Refunds

Getting money back after cancellation takes days or weeks. Contacting customer support is slow, confusing, and stressful.

👥 Old ticketing websites leave everyday users stressed and frustrated.

04 — Requirements

System Requirements

What the website does (Features) and what tools it needs to run

⚙️

Key Features of the Website

  • Easy Search & Filter: Find trips or events quickly by date, price, departure time, and city.
  • 7-Minute Seat Hold: When you click a seat, it is reserved for you for 7 minutes so nobody steals it.
  • Secure QR Ticket: A verified QR code appears on your phone screen that gate staff scan in 1 second.
  • Simple UPI & Card Payments: Pay instantly using Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, Debit Card, or Net Banking.
  • 1-Click Cancel & Transfer: Easily send your ticket to a friend or cancel it to get an automatic refund.
  • Admin Control Center: Organizers can view total bookings, live revenue, and scanner entry logs.
🛡️

Performance & Software Needs

  • Super Fast Loading: Pages open in under 1 second, even when thousands of users are booking at once.
  • Zero Double Booking: High-speed memory locking guarantees two people can never book the same seat.
  • Safe & Private Data: User passwords, personal info, and payment records are fully protected.
  • Works on Any Device: Runs smoothly on any smartphone, iPad/tablet, or laptop with no app install needed.
Minimum Setup: User needs any phone or laptop with Chrome or Edge browser. Server runs on Node.js, React, PostgreSQL database, and Redis cache.
05 — How It Works

System Architecture

A simple look at how the screens, servers, and database work together

📱 1. User Screens (Frontend)
React.js Website  •  Mobile-Friendly Responsive UI  •  Admin Sales Dashboard  •  Gate Ticket Scanner App
⚙️ 2. Server & Logic (Backend)
User Login & Security  •  Search & Filtering Engine  •  7-Minute Seat Locker  •  Dynamic QR Code Maker  •  Payment Verifier
🗄️ 3. Storage & Fast Memory
PostgreSQL (Saved Bookings & Users)  •  Redis (Super-Fast Seat Locks)  •  Cloud Storage (PDF Tickets)  •  Audit History Logs
🔌 4. External Services
UPI & Card Gateways (PhonePe, Cards)  •  SMS & Email Alerts (Twilio)  •  Airline & Train Partner Data  •  Entry Turnstile Gates
⚡ Simple Summary: Every part is connected smoothly so seats are never double-booked and the website never crashes.
